Each scenario falls primarily under one of: HIPAA, PCI DSS, FERPA, or the organization's Internal Governance Policy. Pick the strongest fit.

    Governance takeaway

    Strong cybersecurity governance is not the absence of risk — it is the presence of clear ownership, prioritized controls, and evidence of compliance. Today's exercise mirrors the discipline real organizations use to balance operational continuity, privacy obligations, regulatory duties, and organizational risk.
